# The Bigger, Deeper World with Caves Implementation Plan

**Status: plan for the parent to review (2026-11-08). Built AFTER Village step 2 (eating and the farm) has merged, and BEFORE villagers that wander (spec 7i), grumps and friendly weapons (7f) and the Wizard's tower (7g). Build in chunks, each reviewed (Claude builds, Codex reviews; Codex builds only small pure-logic tasks; Claude is the fallback builder and reviewer if Codex is out of allowance). Docs only: this PR contains no product code.**

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The world doubles in each direction and gets a deep underground. The map grows from 96 x 96 to **192 x 192** blocks and from 20 to **68** blocks high. His whole familiar world (meadow, pond, hill, village, farm, pasture, cottage) sits unchanged in the middle, with new meadows, hills, ponds and trees on every side. Below it are two **always-lit, creature-free cave layers** (shallow caves with glowing mushrooms, deeper crystal caves with gems), a bedrock floor, **gems in the walls** that he digs out (the same Gem item the wizard accepts), **three hidden treasure rooms** with a chest each, and **one old ruined base** deep down to explore and fix up. He gets in by **digging down** or by walking in through a **cave mouth** (six of them); a pale cobble path along the cave floor, lamps, a big **Home button** and Pip always lead him back up. Falling never hurts. His old saved games are shifted, not lost: every block he built keeps its place relative to the meadow.

**Architecture:** Same layering as before. Pure, DOM-free logic in `src/core/` (bounds and offset, world view, caves, treasure, save v3 and migration) and `src/game/` (home lift, Pip leads home, chest opening, gentle drops) is unit-tested with Vitest on the real generated world. The key idea that keeps risk low: **the whole existing 96 x 96 x 20 generator runs unchanged against a shifted view of the big world** (`ShiftedWorld`, offset +48 on every axis), so the old region is identical by construction and every later plan that stamps in meadow coordinates (the wizard's tower) keeps its numbers. The new outer ring and the caves are generated by new, separate, seeded streams, so nothing inside the old region moves. Rendering moves from 16 x 16 x 20 chunks to **16 x 16 x 16 sections** (cheaper to rebuild per dig than today), built lazily, and underground sections are only drawn when the camera is underground or near a cave mouth. Generation runs in a **Web Worker** behind the loading screen. Saves go to a **new key** (`blockpals.save.v3`); the v1 and v2 keys are never written.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5 (strict), Vite 8, Three.js 0.187, Vitest 5, vite-plugin-pwa 2, Playwright 1.65, Web Audio API. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-block-pals-design.md` (section 4 anti-goals, 5 constraints, 7a gestures, 7b item 4 underground bases, 7c camera, 7d Village, 7g wizard, and the new 7h this plan builds).

**Dependencies (must be merged to `main` first):**
- **Village step 2** (eating and the farm; branch `feat/village-step2`): this plan was written against its real code (item model `StackId`, `Farm`, `Herd`, `GEN_VERSION = 2`, save v2 with optional fields, `chunkCull.ts` view-depth rule, `CameraRig`, `lead.ts`). It does not start before step 2 is on `main`. If step 2 changes any signature named here before it merges, the first thing the implementer does is re-read these files and adjust the task text.
- The wizard's tower and grumps plans are **not** dependencies: they are built after this and need the small edits listed in "Knock-on edits to later plans".

## Global Constraints

**Decisions confirmed by the parent (2026-11-08), binding, recorded verbatim:**
- MAP SIZE: 192 x 192 blocks (was 96 x 96).
- DEPTH: about 48 blocks of depth below the surface (world height about 68; today 20 with ground at about y 5 to 9 and bedrock at 0): two cave layers, shallow caves with glowing mushrooms and deeper crystal caves with gems; bedrock floor.
- CAVES: always LIT (glowing crystals and mushrooms; NEVER dark: a dark cave is a failure), NO creatures, easy way back up at all times (a clear route and marker, and Pip can lead him back; never trapped: a "go home" rescue rule like the pit lift), reached by digging down (and some natural cave mouths and entrances on hillsides), nothing scary (sizes, sounds quiet, no sudden drops with damage; falling never hurts).
- DISCOVERIES (the "mix"): glowing crystals and mushrooms light the way; shiny GEMS in the walls that he digs out (these are the gems the wizard accepts, item 123 in the grumps plan: define how cave gems and grump gems share the item and any sell price notes only); a few HIDDEN TREASURE ROOMS each with a chest holding something nice (seeds, gems, or one mythical block from the wizard's list: decide and justify; chests are opened by a tap, contents delivered to the bag, never lost, one-time per chest, persisted); and ONE OLD RUINED BASE deep down (bricks and planks, lit by lamps, a few furnishings) for him to explore and fix up. This answers his wish to "find and build underground bases".
- His old saved games must be shifted: adding depth changes y coordinates and the larger map may change x and z; his existing builds must keep their relative places and survive (the original v1 and v2 save keys are untouched; backups; a NEW save version and key as in step 1; migration idempotent; legacy fixtures plus a hand-built save with edits at the extremes; rollback safe).

**Standing rules (spec and earlier steps; unchanged):**
- British English in every string and voice line. Lines: at most 12 words, four variants per key, no cheer or sound words (`yum|mmm|aww|ooh|hmm|ta-?da|hup|whee|yay|hooray`), no "tap/click/touch" in Pip's lines, none containing `scary|scared|dark|afraid|hurt|lost|wrong|fail`, each line true wherever it can fire, no promises of later steps. Every spoken line also has a picture. Digits only up to 20.
- **Nothing dark, nothing scary, nothing hurt, nothing lost. A dark or scary cave is a failure even if the code works.** No creatures underground. No sound louder or lower than the existing sfx; cave ambience, if any, is a very quiet synthesised shimmer (optional, off by default).
- Always daytime. Sky, fog and light never go black: underground the fog and ground light shift to a **brighter, softer** colour (Task 11), never darker.
- No network calls at runtime, no analytics, no links out, no new dependencies. Works offline.
- Target device: Galaxy Tab A8 (1920 x 1200, Unisoc T618), landscape, Chrome. A steady 30 fps or better. Pixel ratio capped at 1.5. No real-time shadows, **no point lights** (glow is bright tiles, as the Lamp).
- Touch targets at least 88 CSS px. **Quick tap with the hand walks, press and hold (about half a second) digs, drag turns the view (7a, 7c). This plan changes none of their timings, thresholds or meaning.** It adds one new thing a quick tap can land on (a closed chest) and one new button (Home).
- Never write a child's real name anywhere (code, tests, lines, commits, PR text). "Our player", "he".
- **Save:** version 3 under the new key `blockpals.save.v3`. `blockpals.save.v1` and `blockpals.save.v2` are never written, changed or deleted by this build (only the grown-ups' reset removes them). Existing block ids (0 to 39) and item ids (100 to 110) never change; new block ids are appended (56 to 63) and the Gem item is 123.
- Everything he builds is real. Only bedrock and water cannot be dug (and a closed chest, which opens instead).
- Block ids live in a `Uint8Array`: ids stay below 256.

## Review Focus

- **Losing his saved game (the main review).** Every coordinate in an old save moves by exactly +48 on all three axes, once. Check: the shift covers edits, player, crops, trees and animals; the chain v1 to v2 to v3 is pure and idempotent; v1 and v2 keys are byte-identical afterwards; a backup of the raw v2 payload exists; a damaged v3 is copied before anything replaces it; a stored save with a higher version is never overwritten. Tests: Tasks 1, 2, 6, 24.
- **Old region identical:** every old cell (apart from a short documented list) is the same block at +48 (Tasks 1, 4, 18).
- **Dark or scary:** every cave cell is within 6 blocks of a glowing block; every tile used underground is bright even on the darkest face; the fog and ground light underground are lighter than outdoors, not darker; no entity exists underground; no sound is new except a soft chest chime (Tasks 11, 12, 14, 22).
- **Trapped or lost:** every cave room, treasure room and the ruin is reachable on foot (no drops) from a mouth, and the way back is a lit cobble path; the Home button works from any depth; Pip never gets left behind; a hole dug through a cave ceiling can be walked down (gentle drops) and is never a dead end (Tasks 7, 8, 15, 21).
- **Tablet performance:** 2.5 million cells is 14 times the old world. `pathTowards` would allocate about 12 MB per tap at this size; the mesher scans every cell; the world must be generated off the main thread (Tasks 3, 7, 9, 10, 11, 23).
- **Gestures unchanged (7a):** a tap never digs; hold digs; the cell under his or Pip's feet cannot be dug; a closed chest is the only new tap target and a tap on it never builds (Task 19).
- **Public repo:** no real name, no private data in fixtures.

## Decisions made in this plan

Marked **(my call)** where the spec or the parent left room. The parent can overrule any of these; the most important are repeated as questions at the end.

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| World size | **192 x 68 x 192** (the parent's numbers). 2,506,752 cells = 2.39 MiB as a `Uint8Array` (the old world was 184 KB). |
| Where the old world sits | **Offset +48 on x, y and z (my call):** old cell (x, y, z) becomes (x+48, y+48, z+48). The old 96 x 96 area is the exact middle of the new map, with a 48-block ring of new land on every side. Spawn (24, 24) becomes (72, 72): **71 blocks** from the nearest new edge. Justification versus extending only east and south: (1) east and south only would leave his cottage (x 14..20) and spawn 14 to 24 blocks from the west and north walls, with all the new land on one side, so he would meet the wall on half the compass; (2) the y shift is unavoidable anyway, so the migration is already a "shift every coordinate" function and adding x and z costs one line and the same tests; (3) all later stamps (tower, farm zone) stay meadow-relative because generation runs on a shifted view. The price: every saved x and z changes, which is why Tasks 1, 2 and 6 come first and are the hardest-tested. |
| Vertical layout | Bedrock plane y = 0. Old meadow ground (old y 5 to 11) is now **y 53 to 59** (`SURFACE_BASE_Y = 53`). The old bedrock row (old y = 0, now y = 48) becomes Stone so he can dig past it. Deep crystal caves: floors y 8..16, rooms 7 to 10 high. Shallow mushroom caves: floors y 38..42, rooms 5 to 8 high. **Nothing is carved above y = 48 except cave mouths (`CARVE_MAX_Y = 48`)**, so no cell of the old region at y >= 49 changes and a cave never sits under his old pits closer than 1 block. There is always at least 5 blocks of rock (y 49..53+) between the surface and a cave ceiling. |
| Depth | "About 48 blocks": 48 blocks are added below the old bottom; from the meadow top (53) to bedrock (0) is 53 blocks. |
| World height | 68 = 20 + 48 so every roof and the wizard's later tower (top at old y 19 = y 67) fit unchanged. |
| Generation structure | Seven stages, each its own seeded stream, none consuming another's: (0) deep fill (whole layers at once); (1) the old region, **the unchanged existing generator run on `ShiftedWorld`** (old streams `seed`, `seed ^ 0x5eed`); (2) outer ring terrain (`seed ^ 0x0071e2`); (3) outer trees and edge ring (`seed ^ 0x7ee5`); (4) caves (`seed ^ 0xca7e5`, sub-streams per step); (5) treasure rooms and the ruin; (6) markers, lights, ore. `GEN_VERSION` goes 2 to **3**. |
| Documented differences from the old world | The complete list the compare test allows: (D1) old row y = 0 (now 48) Stone instead of Bedrock, or cave air where a cave reaches it; (D2) the footprints of the two stamped cave mouths in the old region (hill flank and quarry); (D3) nothing else. New trees stay at least 4 cells outside the old rectangle. |
| Outer terrain | The old height formula keeps rolling outward (`heightAt(seed, lx, lz)` with negative or large meadow coordinates; the value noise already handles them) so there is no seam. Added only outside the old rectangle plus 6: 5 to 7 extra hills (same cone shape as the old hill, slope at most 1 per block, peak at most y 59), 3 to 4 still ponds (sand bank, static water, no flow), and a **rolling edge**: within 7 blocks of the world edge the ground rises gently up to +3 (at most y 60) so the world ends in soft hills, with a thick ring of trees and the soft invisible wall 1 block inside the edge (unchanged rule), and fog hides the rest. Trees: edge ring of 24 anchors per side, plus a scatter of about 70 trees, spacing at least 5, none within 4 of the old rectangle. |
| Water | Static water blocks only (no flow; water flow is a later step). Pond depth and bank copy the old pond. |
| Caves, shape | **Rooms plus worm tunnels on a coarse graph, no 3D noise (my call)**: bounded cost, deterministic, no per-cell noise over 2.5 M cells. Shallow layer: 24 rooms (radius 5..9, 5..8 high, spaced at least 16 apart), deep layer: 18 rooms (radius 6..11, 7..10 high). Rooms are joined by a minimum spanning tree plus 3 extra loops per layer (so there are loops, not only dead ends). Tunnels are **5 wide x 5 high**, floor slope at most 1 block per 2 cells, then a floor-repair pass guarantees every neighbouring floor pair differs by at most 1 and 3+ blocks of headroom (so Pip and the camera always fit). |
| Getting between layers | Three long stair tunnels (5 x 5, 1 block down per 2 cells, about 50 cells each, crystal-lit) from shallow rooms to deep rooms. |
| Cave mouths | **Six**: two stamped into the old region (the hill's south-west flank near spawn, about 20 blocks away; and the quarry's west wall by Captain Pebble's shop, whom the spec says "leads him to underground bases") and four in the new land, one per side, found by a deterministic slope search. Each is a 5-wide, 5-high doorway with a Lamp on each side, then a walk-in ramp (floor down 1 per 2 cells) to the nearest shallow room. On flat ground the first cells are an open stair-trench, so a mouth is always a visible way down. A cave mouth is the only place anything is carved above y = 48. |
| Lighting (the rule) | Every cave air cell is within **6 blocks (straight line)** of a glowing block (Glowing mushroom in shallow caves, crystals in deep caves, Lamps on the ruin and trail). The generator places lights on a 3-cell grid pass then fixes any gap; **a test scans every carved cell of four seeds** and fails if any is farther than 6. There are no point lights: glow is a bright tile, like the Lamp, so cost is zero. |
| Why "dark" cannot sneak in through rendering | The scene is lit by hemisphere and sun light with no shadows or occlusion, so rock does not shade a cave. Underground the fog colour and ground light are lerped to **lighter** values (Task 11) and a test fixes their luminance floors. |
| The way back | (a) A **cobble trail** (PathStone, the same stone as the village path, 3 wide) runs along the floor from every room to the nearest mouth or stair, with a Lamp in the floor beside it every 6 cells: follow the pale path up. (b) A big **Home button** (house with up arrow, 96 px) appears whenever he is 7 or more blocks below the ground and stays until he is within 5; one tap lifts him to the nearest open surface through a soft white-gold sparkle fade (0.4 s out, 0.4 s in, never black). (c) Pip offers to **lead him home** along the trail after a quiet spell underground. (d) A failed walk tap while deep makes the Home button wobble and Pip say the way-up line instead of the usual "no path" hint. (my call) |
| Falling | There is no falling today (steps are at most 1 block). This plan adds **gentle drops (my call)**: he and Pip can walk off an edge and glide down up to **12 blocks**, never hurt, no impact sound, a soft landing sparkle at most; walking back up is never needed because the Home button and the trail exist. Why: without it, digging through a cave ceiling from above leaves him on a ledge above a drop he cannot step down (a dead end, the exact frustration 4 forbids). Animals, villagers and later grumps use the old 1-block step rule, so nothing wanders into a hole. |
| Digging down | Unchanged: hold digs; the cell under his or Pip's feet stays undiggable; bedrock stops him. A staircase down takes two holds per level (about 1.5 s with the step), so the shallow caves are about 12 levels (18 s) below the meadow: the mouths are the easy way and the staircase is the fun way. Nothing falls on him (there are no gravity blocks) and nothing can bury him. |
| Gems | `GemOre` (block 60) in cave walls, only on cells touching cave air so every one is visible. Digging gives **one Gem item (id 123)** to the bag (all or nothing at 99) and leaves air. **The Gem item is added by this plan** (same definition the grumps plan lists: picture "💎", kind `gift`, not edible, not feed, not placeable); the grumps plan's Task 1 then only adds the weapons 120 to 122. Cave gems and grump gems are one stack with one id, indistinguishable and equally good for the wizard (1 or 2 per bundle) and, later, for Captain Pebble. Counts: at least 14 ore in the shallow layer and 36 in the deep layer, 12 more gems in chests, so about 60+ in total, a finite supply that is plenty for all eight wizard blocks (12 gems buys one bundle of each). **Sell price note for step 3 (note only, not built here):** treat gems as plentiful (about 60 findable plus grumps), so Pebble's buy price should stay small (suggest 2 coins) and the wizard's 1 to 2 gem bundles stay as planned. |
| Treasure rooms | **Three hidden rooms plus the ruin's chest = four chests (my call).** Each room is 7 x 7 x 5, off a main cave room by a short lit 3-wide side tunnel with a DoorFrame archway (found by the glow, never behind a wall), furnished with a Planks table under the chest and two glow blocks. `T0` shallow "seed nook" (3 wheat, 3 carrot, 3 potato seeds and 1 apple sapling); `T1` shallow "gem gallery" (3 Gems and 4 Lamps); `T2` deep "crystal vault" (4 Gems and 4 each of the three crystal kinds); `R` the ruin's chest (5 Gems, 10 Planks, 10 Bricks, 4 red wool, so he can fix the base up). |
| Mythical block in a chest? | **No (my call).** (1) Mythical blocks do not exist yet when this is built (the wizard comes later); (2) the wizard's first welcome gift, trades and puzzles are his reason to visit the tower, and a free one in a chest would weaken that; (3) chest contents must be a fixed table so old saves always agree. The crystals, gems and seeds are the nice things; if the parent wants one mythical block in the deepest chest, the wizard step can add it to `CHEST_REWARDS.R` in one line (a question at the end). |
| Chests | `Chest` (62) closed and `ChestOpen` (63). A quick tap on a closed chest in pick reach opens it (far: he walks beside it first, as for other blocks); the contents go to the bag **all or nothing** (if anything would not fit under 99 the chest stays closed and Pip says the bag-full line: nothing is lost, he can come back); the block becomes `ChestOpen`, which is an ordinary world edit, so it persists and can never pay out twice. A closed chest cannot be dug or carried (so it cannot be lost or duplicated); an open chest is a harmless decoration he may dig and place. A picture overlay (big cards "icon x n") shows what came in and closes by itself after 4 s or on any tap. |
| The ruin ("The Old Hideout") | Stamped like a house, in the deep layer at the world centre: a hall 17 x 13 (inside) with a ruined brick ring (Bricks and MossyBricks, 3 to 4 high, at least 3 wide gaps and ragged missing blocks), a Planks floor patch, 4 floor-standing Lamp posts (Fence plus Lamp), a table (Fence post and Planks top), a bench, a red wool rug, a shelf of Planks, and the chest. The cave ceiling is its roof. No door to fix: he can wall the gaps and add to it, all real blocks. It joins the cave network by two wide tunnels (so it has two ways in). |
| Ids and art | Block ids 56 `GlowMushroom`, 57 `CrystalBlue`, 58 `CrystalPink`, 59 `CrystalGreen`, 60 `GemOre`, 61 `MossyBricks`, 62 `Chest`, 63 `ChestOpen`; 64 to 71 reserved. **48 to 55 are left free on purpose** because the wizard plan's mythical blocks (it says 36 to 43, but step 2 already used 36 to 39) must now start at 40 and its reserved spare is 48 to 55. Crystals, mushroom and ore are full cubes (no new mesher shapes). `BlockDef` gains `light?: boolean` (true for GlowMushroom and the three crystals; the wizard plan reuses it). |
| Mesh sections | 16 x 16 x 16 sections, 5 per column (y 0..15, 16..31, 32..47, 48..63, 64..67). The old surface (y 48..67) is exactly sections 3 and 4. A dig rebuilds the one section containing it (plus a neighbour on an edge): **4096 cells instead of today's 5120**. The mesher reads cells by index arithmetic inside a section and only calls `world.get` on the section border, and emits **no faces on the four outside walls** of the world (never visible from inside, and they would cost 90,000 triangles at this size). |
| Draw rule | A section is drawn only if (a) it passes the existing view-depth rule (`FOG_FAR` 40 along the camera's forward) and (b) the vertical zone rule: outdoors only sections with y >= 48; underground (camera below the local ground by more than 1, or within 24 blocks of a cave mouth) sections whose y-range overlaps camera y +/- 20. Fails open (every section) on bad numbers. |

If `Worker` is missing or throws, the same function runs on the main thread (still works, just a still loading screen). The generator is the same pure `generateWorld(seed)` the tests call. |
| Loading | At load only the ~60 sections within the fog radius of spawn are meshed before the first frame; the rest are built lazily at most 2 per frame, nearest first. |
| Save v3 | `version: 3`, `gen: 3`, plus `origin: [48, 48, 48]` (where meadow cell 0,0,0 sits, written always, informational: a v3 save is never shifted again), `migratedFrom?: 1 | 2`, and flags `cavesSeen`, `ruinFound` (booleans). Chests need no field (the open chest is an edit). New hint names `digDown`, `gemFound`. Reading: v3 first; else v2 (raw copied once to `blockpals.save.v2.backup`, shifted, written to v3); else v1 (raw copied once to the v1 backup as before, migrated, shifted, written to v3). Nothing ever writes the v1 or v2 key. |
| Pip underground | Pip follows with the existing rules (he teleports to him when 10+ blocks away, 4+ blocks above or below, stuck, or after a Home lift). Underground he uses the same follow pathing with gentle drops allowed. He leads home along the trail on request (Task 21). |
| Villagers, animals | Stay on the surface. Test: no entity y is below ground minus 3. |
